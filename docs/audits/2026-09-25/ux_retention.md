# Books & Runs: UX / IA / Onboarding / Retention Audit (2026-09-25)

Scope: IA, first-run, Home, navigation, meta-progression, retention, economy, profile/cosmetics, achievements, help, settings, error/offline, microcopy. Replay/spectator and native packaging excluded. Read AGENTS.md and CODEBASE_MAP.md (§8, §9) first. The Third Scorecard, prior UX audit and S-tier audit are treated as closed. Live checks: preview dev server, mobile 375x812 and desktop 1280x800, guest/first-run state only (no throwaway account signed in, so signed-in Home and Profile findings come from code).

## 1. Executive summary

The product is unusually complete for a solo-dev PWA: guided tutorial, first-session softening of Home CTAs, daily and weekly seeded content with streaks and friend boards, 220 achievements with rarity, a rich cosmetic catalog, async MP with push and nudge, 10 languages, and strong a11y and settings sync. Most "table stakes" from Wordle/Uno/Words With Friends are met.

The gaps are about the retention loop, not features:

1. **Guests get no progression at all.** XP, levels, achievements and stats are server-verified and account-only. A guest who finishes a full game sees "Not saved. Sign in". Wordle, Solitaire apps and Candy Crush show local progress first and ask for an account later.
2. **Daily Deal and Weekly Challenge, the habit loops, award no XP, achievements or level progress.** They give only a streak number and a friend board (GameOverScreen.tsx:318-334 skips recording for daily/weekly).
3. **There is no short-cycle goal layer.** No quests, weekly goals, seasons, battle-pass track or "next reward" preview. XP is a quadratic level curve with cosmetic milestones at levels 5/10/25/50…, but the next milestone is not surfaced on Home.
4. **Time-to-first-game is 4 taps and about 6 screens of decisions** (Home, New Game, Solo, setup with Start below the fold, then Medium AI and 7 rounds by default). There is no "Play now" one-tap default.
5. **Desktop is a 448px column** (`max-w-md`) on a 1280px screen; the Home screen is a long vertical list.
6. **Push is one generic toggle with generic English-only copy.** There is no per-category control or per-game context, and no install (A2HS) prompt.

None of these are defects. They are the difference between "polished" and "sticky".

## 2. Strengths (already at or above modern standard, do not re-litigate)

- **Interactive tutorial**: 15 gated coach-mark steps on a scripted deal, spotlighted targets, skip control. Promoted to a card only for first-time sessions (firstSessionStore.ts, new-game/page.tsx:102). Better than most card-game apps (Uno!, Solitaire apps have static help).
- **First-session softening** of Sign-in/Daily/Weekly CTAs so New Game stays the primary action; a single filled amber CTA on Home.
- **Home "Your games"** unifies local save, MP games (your turn first, waiting dimmed, stale-day flags) and invites with inline Accept/Decline. Comparable to Words With Friends' games list, with better staleness cues.
- **Cloud-aware Resume**: checks for a newer cloud save before continuing (page.tsx:659).
- **Daily Deal and Weekly Challenge**: seeded and shareable, with a friend leaderboard for Daily, "streak protected" copy, and streak-at-risk push (daily-deal-reminder). Ahead of most indie games.
- **Achievements**: 220 across 44 families/5 tiers, filter/sort, a "Closest" sort, "only 4% of players" rarity, and a Home "closest achievement" card with a progress bar. Comparable to Steam's rarity percentage.
- **Game-over XP breakdown**, level-up sound and pulse, and a cosmetic UnlockToast.
- **Cosmetics**: a real rarity system (common to apex), foil/rings, locked-item convention (dim, never hide, with 🔒 caption), public Trophy Case, share cards. Boutique is honestly gated ("creator" today), with no dark-pattern purchasing.
- **Economy ethics**: no premium currency, no loot boxes, no ads, no energy timers. Tip page is optional and clearly labeled. This is a genuine differentiator vs Candy Crush and Marvel Snap.
- **Account-sync of every preference**, account-switch data isolation, and a Settings tab structure with a per-tab reset.
- **Accessibility**: colorblind mode, text scale, reduced motion, focus trap, 38 themes with fixed contrast, haptics.
- **10 languages** and offline shell, update banner, and error reporting.
- **Empty-state component**, PageTip first-visit tips and "Show again" in Settings.
- **Review prompt** after a real win only, with "not really" routed privately.
- **Welcome dialog** does a soft-ask for push (good pattern) plus language picking.

## 4. Quick wins (each S, roughly 1 day or less)

1. XP bar plus "next unlock: Level N cosmetic" on Home; replace the hover-only tooltip (#3a, #19).
2. First-session New Game: primary CTA is Start tutorial; default new-player setup to Short/Easy; sticky Start button; avoid stacked tips (#4).
3. Replace native `confirm()` with a shared dialog (#12).
4. Show display name, avatar and level on Home instead of the email (#19).
5. One-line Boutique expectation copy (#22).
6. Hide "Whose turn is it?" in solo (#23).
7. Daily/Weekly game-over: show streak plus "+XP" once #2 is decided.
8. Personalize push copy with opponent name (server-side string change, needs `mp` redeploy) (#6 partial).
9. Card diagrams in How to Play (#17).
10. "Equip now" on UnlockToast (#10 partial).

## 5. Bigger bets

1. **Guest progression with claim-on-sign-up** (#1, plus #8 anonymous-upgrade): biggest activation and conversion lever.
2. **Rewarded daily/weekly loops plus quests/season track** (#2, #3): converts the existing content into a return-daily habit without paywalls, and stays consistent with the current ethical economy. Needs a server-side XP credit path and a quest definition table (derive from `replayStats` deltas so it stays anti-cheat consistent).
3. **Wider desktop layout plus persistent navigation** (#5, #18): a nav shell (bottom tabs on mobile, side rail on desktop).
4. **Profile hub refactor** (#10, #11, #14): split the 2262-line page into Overview/Collection/Stats with equip-from-unlock and reveal moments.
5. **Notification system v2** (#6, #7, #9): categories, localized personalized copy, quiet hours, welcome-back and install nudges.
6. **Passwordless/social sign-in** (#8).
7. **Just-in-time in-game hints and glossary** (#16) replacing part of the long tutorial.
